# Product or feature launch

A launch shows innovation and commitment to solving customer pain points. The plan should lead with how it helps users and where it moves the company, and keep the message consistent across every channel.

## Ask first

- What it is, who it is for, and the problem it solves
- Why it was built (customer feedback, market opportunity) and whether it is the start of something bigger, like a v2
- Rollout timing, what is available now, and any early access or demo links
- Any beta users or early customers willing to be quoted
- Whether outside partners should get ready-to-post copy

## Core checklist items

**Prep and approvals**
- Messaging doc. Needs: one sentence on the news plus approved facts. Owner: founder.
- Internal FAQ for employees. Needs: talking points for common questions and what is new, different, and important about the launch, so the team can discuss it with customers and partners. Owner: founder or product lead.
- Customer newsletter. This is a core item for launches, unlike most other announcements. Needs: a brief introduction, how it meets customer needs, and a link to details, demos, or early access. Owner: founder or marketing.
- Images. Needs: screenshots with overlays showing the feature in action, a graphic with the feature name and a tagline about the benefit (for example "Unlock new efficiency with [Feature]"), and graphics employees, partners, and stakeholders can share. Customer quotes or case study images from beta users help if available. Keep branding consistent.
- Short personal copy for employees about their role in bringing it to life, and ready-to-use copy for partners and stakeholders so they can post on their own channels. Owner: founder, with timing coordinated with internal teams and partners.

**Day of**
- Share the internal FAQ.
- LinkedIn posts from the founder and the brand page. Employees share their own.
- Send the customer newsletter.

## Optional items (offer one at a time)

- **Blog post.** Useful for a larger launch where people need a link for details: what is changing, when it rolls out, and what the feature enables. Needs: why it was built, key features and benefits, and the vision for what comes next.
- **Stakeholder newsletter.** For a major launch only. Otherwise fold it into the monthly investor update. Needs: overview, how it strengthens the market position, and thanks for stakeholder support.
- **Press release,** only if the launch is genuinely newsworthy for local or trade media, with a pitch to those outlets if so.
