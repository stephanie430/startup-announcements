# Partnership announcement

The partner may want to take part, amplify the news, post their own version, or simply approve yours. Either way, the plan should get timing and messaging agreed in a way that leaves them comfortable and fully on board. Their sign-off comes before anything else in the checklist.

## Ask first

- Who the partner is, what the partnership actually does, and who benefits (the company, the partner, customers)
- Whether the partner has approved the announcement, and whether they plan to post or want to review materials
- Whether the partner has a comms or PR team and brand guidelines
- Whether the partnership changes anything for customers, which decides if a customer newsletter makes sense
- Target date, and how flexible it is

## Core checklist items

**Prep and approvals**
- Partner sign-off on the news and on timing. Suggest a timeline, but be ready to work with theirs. Owner: founder.
- Messaging doc. Needs: one sentence on the news, approved facts, the value of the partnership, and its likely business impact. Owner: founder.
- If the partner has a comms team, send them final versions of everything and agree on what to post and when. Owner: founder and partner.
- Images. Needs: a graphic with both logos or a photo of the people involved, following the partner's brand guidelines and with their approval. A second image stating the vision and what the partnership enables is a good addition. Owner: founder.
- Short personal copy for employees. Give them a link or image to point to and encourage them to put it in their own words. Owner: team.

**Day of**
- LinkedIn posts from the founder and the brand page, tagging the partner. Employees share personal versions.
- Tell the partner the posts are live.

## Optional items (offer one at a time)

- **Blog post.** Needs: explanation of the partnership, how it benefits both sides, and the vision for working together. Not on a Friday.
- **Press release.** Only if the partnership is truly significant and the partner is fine with media outreach. Needs: quotes from the CEO and at least one other stakeholder (investor, partner, or new hire).
- **Customer newsletter.** Only if it truly affects customers by changing the value they get. Needs: introduction of the partner, how the customer experience improves, and links to details.
- **Stakeholder newsletter** for investors, mentors, and advisors, not customers. Needs: the strategic reasons, how it fits long-term goals, and thanks to the people who helped make it happen.
- **Internal FAQ for employees.** Needs: talking points for common questions so the team can discuss it with customers, partners, or media.
- **Pitch to local and trade press,** only if a press release is in the plan.
