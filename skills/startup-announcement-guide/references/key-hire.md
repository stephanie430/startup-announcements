# Key hire announcement

A hire post is a chance to introduce someone and show the company is growing. An image or a get-to-know-you tone works well. This is usually a light plan.

## Ask first

- Name, role, and a short background with one or two real achievements
- Why this hire now (growth, new direction) and what it enables for customers or the mission
- Whether the hire is C-level, since that changes the investor update and press advice
- Whether a headshot and a team photo are available

## Core checklist items

**Prep and approvals**
- Messaging doc. Needs: one or two sentences on the news plus approved facts. Owner: founder.
- Images. Needs: headshot of the new hire and a photo of the team, on brand and sized for LinkedIn. Owner: founder.
- Brand post angle. Needs: brief background and key achievements, the reason for the hire, how it ties to company goals, and how it helps customers or the mission. Owner: founder.
- Short personal copy from employees, especially the leadership team, welcoming the new hire. Owner: team, in their own words.

**Day of**
- Brand LinkedIn post.
- Tell the team internally that it is live, and encourage personal shares tagging the new hire and the company.

## Optional items (offer one at a time)

- **Investor update.** Only for C-level hires, and skip it if the monthly update is about to go out. Needs: the person's experience and what they will enable.
- **Press release or fact sheet.** Getting press on hires is hard until a company is fairly large. C-level hires can sometimes get local or trade coverage. Needs: quotes from the CEO and the new hire.
- **Pitch to local press,** only if a release is in the plan.
